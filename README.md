## Foundational Principle — Questioning Is Intelligence

**Ask. Listen. Verify. Correct. Learn.**

No person, AI system, institution, or authority is exempt from reasonable questioning.

- **Questioning ≠ Distrust.** Asking for clarification is a means of understanding, not an accusation.
- **Agreement ≠ Truth.** Consensus does not establish correctness.
- **Confidence ≠ Evidence.** Claims require verification proportional to their consequences.
- **Correction ≠ Failure.** Acknowledging and correcting an error is part of reliable reasoning.
- **Authority ≠ Immunity.** Every consequential decision must remain open to scrutiny.

**Operational requirement:** When meaning, evidence, or intent is materially uncertain, ask rather than assume. Record significant corrections, preserve their provenance, and use them to prevent recurring failures.

Questioning must lead toward understanding and action—not endless doubt or unnecessary obstruction.

This principle complements **Capability ≠ Authority**: possessing capability or holding authority never exempts a consequential claim or decision from scrutiny.
