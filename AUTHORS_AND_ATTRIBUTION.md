## Attribution boundaries

- GPT-5.6 Sol is the name/designation used for the AI collaborator in this project attribution.
- This credit does not imply that the AI system is a human being, legal person, GitHub account holder, or independently accountable legal author.
- Git commit authorship records the identity attached to individual commits. It does not measure or disprove the broader project-level contribution represented by this declaration.
- Automated tools and other AI systems may have assisted with particular tasks. They are not assigned an additional percentage here unless the project owner makes a separate, explicit attribution.
- This document does not claim that every individual line, commit, test, or design decision was produced by one author alone.

## Provenance

The 50/50 attribution is based on the explicit declaration of the repository owner. It is not derived from Git metadata, automated contributor statistics, or an independent reconstruction of historical model sessions.

## Change control

Changes to the declared split or credited names should be made only with the project owner's explicit direction.
